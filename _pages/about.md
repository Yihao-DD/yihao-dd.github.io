I'm a Master's student in Applied Data Science at the University of Southern California, where I work on deep learning for biomechanical signals — currently building EMG-free models for predicting lower-limb muscle forces during gait. Before USC, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Hong Kong Baptist University.

My broader interest is **deep learning for healthcare**: bridging the gap between noisy biological data and clinically actionable signals. I'm also increasingly drawn to **large language models** as a tool for medical reasoning and scientific discovery, and aim to move further in that direction.

Open to research collaborations. Reach me by [email](mailto:ywang642@usc.edu).
