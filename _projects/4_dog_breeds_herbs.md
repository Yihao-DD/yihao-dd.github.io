Co-developed a Transformer-based fine-grained image classification model that uses **HERBS** — High-temperature Refinement and Background Suppression — to disentangle visually similar dog breeds where conventional CNN features struggle.

I contributed to model training, evaluation, and ablation analysis. The work was published at IEEE ICEACE 2023.
