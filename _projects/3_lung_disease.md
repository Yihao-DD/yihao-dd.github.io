A CT-based detection pipeline for COVID-19 that fuses lung segmentation, moment-based feature extraction, metaheuristic feature selection, and KNN classification.

Manta Ray Foraging Optimization and Differential Evolution were applied to the feature-selection step to control dimensionality and improve generalization on medical image classification.
