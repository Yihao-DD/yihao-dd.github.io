Built a Python pipeline that reconstructs 3D mouse-brain vascular volumes from sequences of plane-wave ultrasound slices. The pipeline handles slice-to-slice registration, artifact correction, and visualization to recover vessel continuity across the volume.

The work supports downstream quantitative analysis of complex biomedical imaging data, with an emphasis on robust spatial alignment under low-SNR conditions.
